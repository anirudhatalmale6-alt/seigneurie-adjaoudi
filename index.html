Discover the House

A living heritage

The Adjaoudi Lordship is a family and cultural identity. It gathers memory, values and projects, and gives them a durable place to live.

It is not a public authority. It exercises no official function, holds no delegated power, and speaks for no state or administration. The word Lordship is used here as a family and cultural designation.

This site is being built in the open. What is not yet established is shown as missing rather than filled in. Every historical entry will carry a date and a source; every portrait and every document will carry a credit and a right of use.

Awaiting authorisation This timeline has no entries yet.

It is empty on purpose. An entry is added only when three things exist together: a date, a source that can be named, and the family’s written authorisation to publish it. A heritage timeline is worth exactly as much as its weakest entry.

What an entry carries

  • Date — Exact or approximate, stated as such.
  • Event — One sentence, factual, without adjectives.
  • Place — Named only if the source names it.
  • Source — Document, register, publication or attributed testimony.
  • Media — Optional, with credit and right of use.
  • Category — Family, place, object, document or initiative.

Editorial selection

Nothing is published here until the family has authorised it in writing, and until the entry carries a date and a source.
