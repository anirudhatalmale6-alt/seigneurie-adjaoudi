Adjaoudi Lordship

House of Adjaoudi

This page will present the branches of the family and the portraits the House has approved. It is empty, and it will stay empty until two conditions are met for each person named.

Two conditions

  • The person, or the family for a person who has died, has authorised publication in writing.
  • What is published is limited to what that authorisation covers — a name, a role, a portrait, nothing beyond.

By default, all private family data is excluded from the public site. That is the House’s own rule, and it is the safer way round: a name is easy to add later and impossible to take back once it has been indexed.

Branches

Awaiting authorisation Nothing is published here until the family has authorised it in writing, and until the entry carries a date and a source.

Approved portraits

No portrait on this site is generated, reconstructed or retouched into something it was not. If an image is ever restored or generated, it will say so on the image itself.
