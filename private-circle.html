Why a reference and not an email address

The form asks for a member reference rather than an email address, and that is deliberate. A sign-in page that accepts an address turns every failed attempt into an answer about who is, and is not, part of this family.

Four things to settle before it opens

  • Who holds the member list, and in which country it is held.
  • Who may invite, and who may withdraw an invitation.
  • What is behind the door: which archives, at which level of detail.
  • How long an access log is kept, and who is allowed to read it.
