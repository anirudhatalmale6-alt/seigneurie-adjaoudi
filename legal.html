Privacy

What this site does today, stated as facts that can be checked in the page source.

  • This site sets no cookie.
  • It runs no analytics and no measurement of any kind.
  • It makes no request to any third party. Fonts, images and styles are served from the site itself.
  • The forms are not connected and transmit nothing.
  • No account exists, so no password and no personal data are stored.

This will change the day a form is connected or the Private Circle opens. On that day this page has to say what is collected, why, for how long it is kept, and who can ask for it back — and it has to say it before the feature is switched on, not after.

Accessibility

  • Target: WCAG 2.2 level AA on every template of this site.
  • Text contrast is measured on the rendered page at fourteen screen widths, not estimated from the stylesheet.
  • Every interactive element is reachable and usable with the keyboard alone, and shows where the focus is.
  • Animation is limited to short transitions and is switched off entirely for anyone who has asked their system to reduce motion.
  • Every image carries a text alternative; decorative shapes are hidden from screen readers rather than described.
